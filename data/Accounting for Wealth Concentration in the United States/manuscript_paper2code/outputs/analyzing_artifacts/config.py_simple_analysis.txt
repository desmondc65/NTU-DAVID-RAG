The `config.py` file serves as the central repository for all parameters, structural constants, and empirical targets required for the Overlapping-Generations (OLG) model. It ensures consistency across the data processing, model solution, and calibration phases by loading values from `config.yaml` and defining the target moments extracted from the research paper.

### 1. Structural Design and Initialization
As per the class diagram, the `Config` class will be initialized with a path to `config.yaml`. 
*   **YAML Parsing**: Use `yaml.safe_load` to ingest raw settings.
*   **Parameter Organization**: Data will be partitioned into two primary dictionaries: `params` (model constants and initial guesses) and `targets` (empirical moments).
*   **Period Conversion**: Since the model operates on 5-year periods (as per `config.yaml`: `annual_to_period_factor: 5`), the `Config` class must handle the conversion of annual rates (depreciation, discount factor, interest rates) into period equivalents.
    *   *Period Discount Factor*: $\beta_{period} = \beta_{annual}^5$.
    *   *Period Depreciation*: $\delta_{period} = 1 - (1 - \delta_{annual})^5$.

### 2. Logic Analysis of Parameters (`params`)
The `params` dictionary must encompass all components described in Section IV of the paper:

*   **Demographics**:
    *   Total periods ($J=16$), Retirement period ($J_r=10$).
    *   Survival probabilities ($s_j$): Coefficients for the logistic function $\text{logit}(s_j) = \omega_0 + \omega_1 j + \omega_2 j^2$ from Halliday et al. (2019).
*   **Preferences**:
    *   Utility function exponents: $\sigma_c = 1.5$, $\sigma_l = 1.22$.
    *   Calibratable parameters: $\theta$ (labor disutility), $\beta$ (discounting), $\phi_1, \phi_2, \sigma_b$ (bequest motive).
*   **Production and Technology**:
    *   $\alpha$: Capital share (targeted to match net labor income share).
    *   $\Psi$: TFP (normalized to 1.55 to achieve $w=1$).
*   **Labor Productivity Process**:
    *   Ordinary states (1-6): AR(1) parameters (persistence 0.97, variance 0.81).
    *   Extraordinary states (7-8): Values for $z_7, z_8$ and transitions $\lambda_{in}, \lambda_{out}$.
    *   Initial distribution: Parameters for $\zeta$ (initial worker distribution).
*   **Return Process**:
    *   States: $\kappa_L, \kappa_H, \kappa_{top}$ and the productivity-dependent transition matrix $\Pi_\kappa(z)$.
*   **Tax and Transfer System**:
    *   $\tau_s = 0.05$ (Sales).
    *   $\tau_c = 0.236$ (Corporate).
    *   Estate tax: 20% rate on the top 2% of estates.
    *   Progressive income tax: Log-linear parameters to match the 12.4% gap between top 1% and bottom 99% (Piketty and Saez, 2007).
*   **Computational Grids**:
    *   Asset grid constants: `size=500`, `min=0`, `max=5000`. Logic requires a non-linear (log-like) distribution to provide high density at low wealth levels and sufficient coverage for the 0.1% tail.

### 3. Logic Analysis of Empirical Targets (`targets`)
The `targets` dictionary stores the moments the `Calibrator` will attempt to match, primarily derived from Tables 1, 3, and 4:

*   **Wealth Concentration**:
    *   Top 0.1% Share: 13%
    *   Top 1% Share: 35%
    *   Top 5% Share: 62%
    *   Gini Coefficient: 0.84 (or 0.81 for non-negative).
*   **Earnings Concentration**:
    *   Top 0.1% Share: 6%
    *   Top 1% Share: 17%
*   **Joint Distribution/Labor Shares** (Section V.A):
    *   Labor share of Top 0.1% Income: 49%
    *   Labor share of Top 1.0% Income: 59%
*   **Macro/Mobility Aggregates**:
    *   Capital-to-Income Ratio ($K/Y$): 3.0.
    *   Bequest-to-Wealth Ratio.
    *   Intergenerational wealth correlation: 0.37 (Charles and Hurst, 2003).

### 4. Interface Logic
The `Config` class must support the `update_params(new_params)` method.
*   **Purpose**: During the calibration loop, the optimizer (Differential Evolution) will propose `trial_params`. This method must update the internal `params` dict, specifically parameters like `beta`, `theta`, `phi1`, `phi2`, and `extraordinary_productivity`.
*   **Validation**: Ensure updated parameters remain within economically meaningful bounds (e.g., $\beta < 1$, $\theta > 0$).

### 5. Data Paths
Consistent with the `DataHandler` design, `config.py` will define the paths for:
*   `SCF_WAVES`: List of CSV files for 2001-2019.
*   `IRS_DATA`: Reference path for Piketty-Saez (2003) update.

This centralized structure ensures that when `OLGEngine` or `EquilibriumSolver` access a parameter (e.g., `config.params['tau_s']`), they are using the exact same value defined by the researcher, facilitating reproducibility.
