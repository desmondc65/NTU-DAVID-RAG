The logic analysis for `main.py` focuses on orchestrating the empirical, computational, and experimental components described in the paper. The primary objective is to quantify the drivers of US wealth concentration through a calibrated life cycle model.

### **Logic Analysis: `main.py`**

#### **1. Initialization and Configuration**
*   **Action:** Load `config.yaml` using the `Config` class.
*   **Logic:** Centralize all exogenous parameters (e.g., `sigma_c`, `sigma_l`, `alpha`, `tax_system`) to ensure consistency across the empirical processor and the GE solver.
*   **Key References:** Use `model_parameters` for demographic setup (16 periods of 5 years) and `tax_system` for initial fiscal settings.

#### **2. Empirical Fact Discovery (`EmpiricalProcessor`)**
*   **Action:** Process SCF (2001–2019) data to derive the targets for model calibration.
*   **Labor Imputation:** Execute the business income regression logic: $\ln(\text{Business Income}_i) = \text{const} + 0.25 \ln(\text{Equity}_i) + \beta \ln(\text{Labor Controls}_i)$. 
    *   Per the paper, assign 75% of income to labor for households in the imputation sample (active owners not reporting salary).
*   **Return Heterogeneity:** Calculate the relative rate of return $r_i/r_0$ for top groups using **Equation (2)**:
    $$\frac{r_i}{r_0} = \frac{e_{i/0}}{k_{i/0}} \cdot \frac{1/\lambda_i - 1}{1/\lambda_0 - 1}$$
    where $\lambda_i$ is the labor share of group $i$.
*   **Output:** A dictionary of `calibration_moments` (top 1% wealth share, earnings shares, bequest-to-wealth ratio).

#### **3. Model Calibration (Internal Loop)**
*   **Action:** Solve for parameters that cannot be set exogenously.
*   **Moments and Parameters:**
    *   **Discount Factor ($\beta$):** Targeted to match $K/Y = 3.0$ (value from `config.yaml`).
    *   **Work Disutility ($\theta$):** Targeted to match 35% of time allocated to work.
    *   **Superearner States ($z_7, z_8$):** Targeted to match top 0.1% and 1% earnings shares.
    *   **Return States ($\kappa$):** Targeted to match the $r_i/r_0$ values calculated in the empirical step.
    *   **Bequest Motive ($\phi_1, \phi_2, \sigma_b$):** Targeted to match the bequest-to-wealth ratio and top 2% estate share.
*   **Mechanism:** Use an optimizer (e.g., `scipy.optimize.minimize`) to minimize the distance between model-generated moments and `calibration_moments`.

#### **4. Benchmarking the US Economy (`EquilibriumSolver`)**
*   **Action:** Solve for the Stationary General Equilibrium.
*   **Nested Loop Logic:**
    1.  **Price Guess:** Start with a guess for aggregate capital $K$, aggregate labor $N$, and government transfers $Tr$.
    2.  **Household Solver:** Pass prices ($w, r, Tr$) to `HouseholdProblem.solve_lifecycle()`.
        *   Use **Endogenous Grid Method (EGM)** for backward induction.
        *   Solve the labor-leisure choice $h$ and consumption-savings $k'$.
    3.  **Simulation:** Use `Simulator.compute_stationary_distribution()` to find the mass of agents across $(j, k, z, \kappa)$.
    4.  **Aggregation:** Calculate aggregate $K_{model}$ and $N_{model}$. Check if government budget balances (tax revenue = $G + Tr + \text{Pensions}$).
    5.  **Convergence:** Update guesses until prices and $Tr$ stabilize.

#### **5. Decomposition Experiments (`ExperimentRunner`)**
*   **Action:** Reproduce Table 5 and Figure 7 by systematically shutting down model channels.
*   **Counterfactuals:**
    *   **Scenario (a) - Equal Bequests:** Set $p_{\Phi} = 0$ or redistribute all $E$ equally, removing the correlation with parental $z$ and $\kappa$.
    *   **Scenario (b) - No Superearners:** Modify the productivity grid such that $z_7 = z_8 = z_6$ (highest ordinary state).
    *   **Scenario (c) - Homogeneous Returns:** Set all $\kappa_i = 1.0$ (the value-weighted average).
*   **Permutation Analysis:**
    *   To calculate **Marginal Effects** (Figure 7 whiskers), run all 3! (6) permutations of removing these factors.
    *   Example: The marginal effect of "Superearners" is calculated as the change in wealth concentration when they are removed from the Benchmark, vs. when they are removed from an economy that already lacks return heterogeneity.

#### **6. Results Generation and Output**
*   **Table 5 Replication:** Extract the Gini coefficient, top 1% wealth share, and top 0.1% wealth share for each counterfactual.
*   **Figure 7 Replication:** Aggregate the marginal effects across permutations to plot the bar heights (averages) and whiskers (ranges).
*   **Life Cycle Verification:** Plot Figure 5 (Age-Wealth profiles) and Figure 6 (Gini by age) to ensure the model aligns with PSID/SCF lifecycle dynamics.

### **Program Call Flow Summary**
1.  **`Config`** loads settings.
2.  **`EmpiricalProcessor`** defines the target landscape.
3.  **`EquilibriumSolver`** iterates using **`HouseholdProblem`** (EGM) and **`Simulator`** (Distribution) until market clearing.
4.  **`ExperimentRunner`** performs the "shutdown" tests.
5.  **`Main`** saves tables and plots.
