[Logic Analysis]
- ['config.py', 'Defines all exogenous parameters (sigma_c=1.5, sigma_l=1.22, theta, delta=0.045) and calibrated grids. Contains the 8x8 labor productivity transition matrix and the 3x3 return transition matrix. Includes tax schedules (corporate 23.6%, estate, progressive income) and demographics (16 periods, survival probabilities).']
- ['utils.py', 'Contains helper functions for interpolation (linear and spline), root-finding for labor supply equations, and the Rouwenhorst approximation for the AR(1) ordinary labor productivity states.']
- ['empirical_processor.py', 'Processes raw SCF data (2001-2019). Implements the labor income imputation: regressing business income on equity with controls to find the 0.25 capital share. Calculates the target moments: top wealth/income/earnings shares and implied returns using the labor share ratio method (Equation 2).']
- ['household_solver.py', "Core engine using the Endogenous Grid Method (EGM). Implements backward induction from period 16 to 1. At each step, it solves the FOC for consumption, savings (k'), and labor (h). Uses Numba decorators to speed up the multi-dimensional interpolation over the asset grid (k), productivity (z), and return (kappa)."]
- ['simulation.py', 'Computes the stationary distribution of households over the state space (age, assets, z, kappa). Uses the policy functions from HouseholdProblem to iterate on the distribution until it reaches a fixed point. Aggregates individual assets and labor units to find total K and N.']
- ['equilibrium_solver.py', 'Outer loop to find General Equilibrium. Iterates on (r, w, Tr) guesses. Calls HouseholdProblem and Simulator. Adjusts prices based on marginal products of the Cobb-Douglas firm (Y = K^alpha N^(1-alpha)) and ensures the government budget balances with respect to transfers (Tr) and pension benefits.']
- ['experiments.py', 'Executes the decomposition analysis. Defines methods to modify the model environment: (a) Equalizing bequests, (b) Removing superearners (setting z7, z8 = z6), and (c) Homogenizing returns. Runs these in all combinations to produce the marginal effects for Figure 7.']
- ['main.py', 'Entry point. Orchestrates the flow: 1. Empirical data processing. 2. Calibration of internal parameters. 3. Benchmarking the economy. 4. Running counterfactuals and generating Table 5 and Figure 7 outputs.']

[Shared Knowledge]
Transition matrices and asset grids are shared across HouseholdProblem and Simulator. The asset grid 'k' should be non-linearly spaced (dense near zero, sparse at high levels) to capture both precautionary savings and the extreme tail. The productivity states (z7, z8) and return states (kappa_top) must be sourced from the Supplemental Appendix B. All modules utilize the same demographic profile (16 periods of 5 years each).
