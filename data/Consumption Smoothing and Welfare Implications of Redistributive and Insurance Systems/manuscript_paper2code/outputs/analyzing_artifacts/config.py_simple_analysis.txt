The `config.py` file serves as the central repository for all parameters and structural settings of the OLG model. Its primary responsibility is to load the `config.yaml` file, perform necessary conversions (especially from annual to 5-year period rates), and define the dimensions and bounds for the numerical grids used in the Value Function Iteration (VFI).

### 1. Demographic and Temporal Structure
*   **Life Cycle Mapping**: The model spans ages 25 to 99. With 1 period = 5 years, the total number of periods $J = 15$.
    *   **Working Periods**: $j \in \{1, \dots, 8\}$ (Ages 25–64).
    *   **Retirement Period**: $j \in \{9, \dots, 15\}$ (Ages 65–99).
    *   **Retirement Threshold ($J_r$)**: Set to 9 (corresponds to age 65).
*   **Time-Aggregation**: 
    *   **Discount Factor ($\beta$):** The `beta_annual` from `config.yaml` must be converted to $\beta_{period} = (\beta_{annual})^5$.
    *   **Depreciation ($\delta$):** The `delta_annual` must be converted to $\delta_{period} = 1 - (1 - \delta_{annual})^5$.

### 2. Preference and Technology Parameters
*   **Utility Constants**:
    *   **$\sigma = 1.8$**: Inverse Frisch elasticity of labor supply.
    *   **$v_g$**: Gender-specific utility constants (`vg_male`, `vg_female`) to ensure positive utility, preventing agents from preferring death over low-health states.
    *   **$\eta = 1.7$**: Equivalence scale for married households (as per OECD scale).
*   **Production**:
    *   **$\alpha = 0.29$**: Capital share of income.
    *   **$\Psi = 1.45$**: Total Factor Productivity (TFP), chosen to normalize the baseline wage $w=1$.
    *   **Target $K/Y$**: 3.2 (used to calibrate $\beta$ in the outer loop).

### 3. Government and Policy parameters
*   **Taxation**:
    *   **$\tau_c = 0.10$**: Flat consumption tax.
    *   **Benabou/Heathcote Progressive Tax**: Parameters $\lambda$ and $\tau$ are stored separately for `tax_single` and `tax_married`. 
    *   **Disposable Income Formula**: $y_d = \lambda y^{1-\tau}$.
*   **Insurance & Pensions**:
    *   **$\phi = 0.20$**: Out-of-pocket medical copayment rate (80% coverage).
    *   **$G\_ratio = 0.116$**: Exogenous government spending as a share of GDP.
    *   **Pension Replacement**: Targets a pension/GDP ratio of 9.5%.

### 4. Health and Productivity
*   **Health Parameters**: $\gamma$ (return to scale) and $H$ (productivity) for medical spending.
*   **Productivity Process**: 
    *   Annual persistence 0.898 and $\sigma_z = 0.77$.
    *   `intra_family_corr = 0.1`: Correlation for married couples' productivity.
    *   `college_premium = 0.34`: Used to scale the permanent component of the productivity grid.

### 5. Numerical Grid Settings (`get_grid_settings()`)
To solve the model accurately via VFI, the following grids must be defined:

| Grid | Type | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asset ($k$)** | Log-spaced | Since utility is concave, a denser grid at lower asset levels is required to capture borrowing constraints ($k \geq 0$). Range: $[0, K_{max}]$. |
| **Avg. Earnings ($\tilde{e}$)** | Linear | Tracks career earnings for pension calculation. Range based on max possible productivity. |
| **Health ($h$)** | Discrete | Exactly 5 states as per Section 4.6 ($h \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$). |
| **Productivity ($z$)** | Markov Chain | 6 states (3 transitory $\times$ 2 permanent) derived via the Rouwenhorst method. |

### 6. Logic Analysis of `config.py` Implementation
*   **Initialization**: The class should read `config.yaml` using a YAML parser and store values in a flattened `params` dictionary for easy access by `EquilibriumSolver` and `HouseholdSolver`.
*   **Validation**: Ensure that $J$, $J_r$, and the period length are consistent with the paper's ages (25-99, retirement at 65).
*   **Interface Fidelity**: `get_grid_settings()` must provide the number of points (e.g., `n_k = 100`, `n_e = 50`) and bounds to the `ParamsGenerator`.
