[Implementation approach]
We will implement a General Equilibrium Overlapping Generations (OLG) model using a nested fixed-point iteration approach. 1. **Calibration**: Pre-calculate deterministic wage profiles and discretize stochastic processes (labor productivity via Rouwenhorst method, health status via transition matrices). 2. **Household Solver**: Implement Value Function Iteration (VFI) with backward induction starting from the maximum age (99). We will utilize Numba-accelerated loops to handle the high-dimensional state space of married couples ($k, h, z_m, z_f, 	ilde{e}$) and use linear interpolation for the asset grid. 3. **Stationary Distribution**: Simulate a large population of agents (e.g., 50,000) or use a non-stochastic distribution method to find the stationary distribution $\Gamma$. 4. **GE Outer Loop**: Solve for the equilibrium interest rate $r$, wage $w$, and government budget (premium rate $t$) using a root-finding algorithm (e.g., Brent's method or Broyden's). 5. **Counterfactuals**: Re-solve the GE under three scenarios (No Pension, No Health Insurance, Linear Tax) and compute compensating variations for welfare analysis and pass-through coefficients for consumption insurance metrics.
