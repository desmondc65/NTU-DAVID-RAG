Based on the research paper and the provided configuration, the `firm_gov.py` module defines the economic environment. It acts as the bridge between aggregate variables ($K, L$) and individual household incentives (taxes, benefits, and factor prices).

### 1. Firm Logic Analysis

The firm operates in a perfectly competitive environment with a Constant-Returns-to-Scale (CRS) Cobb-Douglas production function.

**Production Function:**
$$Y_t = A K_t^{\theta} L_t^{1-\theta}$$
Where:
*   $A$: Total Factor Productivity (`tfp_A`).
*   $\theta$: Capital share of income (`capital_share_theta`).
*   $L_t$: Labor input in efficiency units.

**Factor Prices Calculation (`get_factor_prices`):**
From the firm's profit maximization problem ($\max F(K, L) - (r + \delta)K - wL$):
1.  **Wage Rate ($w$):** The marginal product of labor.
    $$w_t = (1-\theta) A \left( \frac{K_t}{L_t} \right)^\theta$$
2.  **Interest Rate ($r$):** The marginal product of capital minus depreciation.
    $$r_t = \theta A \left( \frac{K_t}{L_t} \right)^{\theta-1} - \delta$$
    *   $\delta$: Depreciation rate (`depreciation_delta`).

### 2. Government: Taxation Logic

The government collects two types of taxes: a progressive income tax and a Social Security payroll tax.

**A. Individual Income Tax (`get_income_tax`):**
The model uses the Gouveia-Strauss (1994) functional form.
*   **Taxable Income ($y$):**
    $$y = \max(r_t a + w_t e_1 h_1 + w_t e_2 h_2 - (1 + \mathbb{1}_{m=0})\xi, 0)$$
    *   $\xi$: Standard deduction and exemptions per adult.
*   **Tax Function:**
    $$\tau_{I,t}(y; m) = \phi_t \left[ y - (y^{-\phi_{m,1}} + \phi_{m,2})^{-1/\phi_{m,1}} \right]$$
    *   $\phi_t$: Scale parameter (`income_tax_phi_t`) adjusted to target 10% GDP revenue.
    *   $\phi_{m,1}, \phi_{m,2}$: Marital status-specific parameters (`income_tax_phi_m1`, `income_tax_phi_m2`).

**B. Social Security Payroll Tax:**
*   Flat tax rate $\tau_P$ (`payroll_tax_tau_p`).
*   Applied to individual earnings ($y_j = w_t e_j h_j$) up to a maximum taxable limit $\vartheta_{max}$.
*   The tax is: $\tau_{P,t}(y_1, y_2) = \tau_P \times [\min(y_1, \vartheta_{max}) + \min(y_2, \vartheta_{max})]$.

### 3. Government: Social Security Benefits (OASI)

The core of the paper is the Primary Insurance Amount (PIA) calculation and the redistribution through spousal/survivors benefits.

**A. PIA Formula:**
The PIA is calculated based on average historical earnings ($b_j$) using three brackets.
*   **Bend Points:** $\vartheta_1, \vartheta_2$ (`pia_bend_points`).
*   **Replacement Rates:** 0.90, 0.32, 0.15 (`pia_replacement_rates`).
*   **Age Adjustment:** Normalized for the cohort's productivity growth since age 60.
    $$\psi(i, b_j) = \mathbb{1}_{i \ge I_R} (1 + \mu)^{60-i} \times \text{ProgressiveFormula}(b_j)$$

**B. Benefit Rules (`get_social_security_benefit`):**
This function must switch logic based on the policy "type" (Baseline vs. Reform).

1.  **Baseline (Current Law):**
    *   **Married ($m=0$):** $tr_{SS} = \psi_t \times \max(\psi(b_1) + \psi(b_2), 1.5\psi(b_1), 1.5\psi(b_2))$.
    *   **Single/Widowed ($m=1, 2$):** $tr_{SS} = \psi_t \times \max(\psi(b_1), \psi(b_2))$. (Survivors benefit is 100% of spouse's PIA).
2.  **Reform (Removed Spousal/Survivors):**
    *   **Married ($m=0$):** $tr_{SS} = \psi_t \times (\psi(b_1) + \psi(b_2))$.
    *   **Single/Widowed ($m=1, 2$):** $tr_{SS} = \psi_t \times \psi(b_j)$.

**C. Marginal Benefits:**
For the household Euler equations, the government module must provide the partial derivatives $\frac{\partial tr_{SS}}{\partial b_1}$ and $\frac{\partial tr_{SS}}{\partial b_2}$ to account for how current work affects future retirement income.

### 4. Government: Budget Balancing and Bequests

**A. OASI Budget Balancing (`balance_ss_budget`):**
The government maintains a separate Social Security budget.
*   **Goal:** Find $\psi_t$ such that $T_{RSS,t}(\psi_t) = T_{P,t} - T_{RO}$.
*   $T_{P,t}$: Total payroll tax revenue.
*   $T_{RO}$: Baseline OASI residual (kept constant).
*   $\psi_t$ is adjusted proportionally for all recipients.

**B. Accidental Bequests ($q_t$):**
*   Wealth remaining from households who die before age 100.
*   Formula: Sum of end-of-period assets $a'$ of deceased households, adjusted for survival rates $\phi_{1,i}, \phi_{2,i}$.
*   Distributed uniformly as a lump-sum transfer to all working-age households ($i < I_R$).

### 5. Alignment with Data Structures
*   **`get_factor_prices(K, L)`**: Uses `tfp_A` and `capital_share_theta` from `config.yaml`.
*   **`get_income_tax(y, m)`**: Implements the Gouveia-Strauss curve.
*   **`get_social_security_benefit(b1, b2, m, type)`**: Handles the switch between current law (with max(1.5*PIA, ...)) and the individual-only benefit reform.
*   **`balance_ss_budget(...)`**: Solves for the scalar $\psi_t$ to ensure the payroll tax covers the benefits plus the baseline residual.
