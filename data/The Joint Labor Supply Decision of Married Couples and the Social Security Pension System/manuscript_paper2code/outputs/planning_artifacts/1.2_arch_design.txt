[Implementation approach]
The software will implement a general-equilibrium overlapping-generations (OLG) model using a modular architecture. We will use a nested-loop approach to solve for the steady-state: an inner loop for the household's dynamic programming problem (backward induction) and an outer loop for market-clearing factor prices (K/L ratio) and government budget balance. For the household problem, we will solve Kuhn-Tucker conditions for labor supply and consumption using a Newton-type solver (scipy.optimize.root) and multi-linear interpolation for wealth and historical earnings. The earning ability AR(1) processes will be discretized using the Rouwenhorst method. Transition paths will be solved by iterating on the entire time-path of factor prices and policy variables, utilizing backward induction over the time dimension and forward simulation of the population distribution. Parallelization via Python's 'multiprocessing' will be used to speed up the solve-time for the large individual state space.
