The `calibration.py` module is responsible for translating the empirical findings described in Section 3 of the paper into the numerical parameters and transition matrices required for the dynastic Aiyagari-Bewley-Huggett model. This involves converting annual data to the model's 5-year period and constructing the composite 4-state labor productivity process.

### 1. State Space Definition for Labor Efficiency ($z$)
The labor efficiency $z$ is composed of an intergenerational fixed effect ($f$) and a life-cycle transitory component ($a$). As per Section 3.2, there are two possible values for each:
- **Intergenerational Component ($f$):** $f \in \{f_L, f_H\} = \{-0.40, 0.55\}$ (from `productivity.f_states`).
- **Life-cycle Component ($a$):** $a \in \{a_L, a_H\} = \{-0.37, 0.33\}$ (from `productivity.a_states`).

The model uses a 4-state discretized grid for $z$, where $z = \exp(f + a)$. The states are ordered as:
1.  **State 1:** $(f_L, a_L) \implies z_1 = \exp(-0.40 - 0.37) \approx 0.463$
2.  **State 2:** $(f_L, a_H) \implies z_2 = \exp(-0.40 + 0.33) \approx 0.932$
3.  **State 3:** $(f_H, a_L) \implies z_3 = \exp(0.55 - 0.37) \approx 1.197$
4.  **State 4:** $(f_H, a_H) \implies z_4 = \exp(0.55 + 0.33) \approx 2.411$

### 2. Time-Scale Conversion
Since the model period is 5 years (`economy.period_years`), parameters provided at an annual frequency in `config.yaml` must be adjusted:
- **Depreciation Rate ($\delta$):** The 5-year depreciation rate is calculated to match the annual rate $\delta_a = 0.08$. 
  $$\delta_{period} = 1 - (1 - \delta_{annual})^5 \approx 0.3409$$
- **Discount Factor ($\beta$):** The 5-year discount factor is calculated from the annual $\beta_a = 0.962$.
  $$\beta_{period} = (\beta_{annual})^5 \approx 0.822$$
- **Life-cycle Transition Matrix ($A$):** The annual transition matrix `productivity.transition_A_annual` ($A_a$) is converted to a 5-year matrix $A_p$ using the matrix power:
  $$A_p = (A_a)^5$$

### 3. Construction of Transition Matrices ($S, D, P$)
The evolution of $z$ depends on whether the agent survives or is replaced by a descendant.

#### A. Survival Transition Matrix ($S$)
If an agent survives (probability $1-\mu$), their $f$ remains fixed, and $a$ transitions according to $A_p$. This results in a block-diagonal 4x4 matrix:
$$S = \begin{pmatrix} A_p & \mathbf{0}_{2x2} \\ \mathbf{0}_{2x2} & A_p \end{pmatrix}$$
This reflects that dynasties in the same $f$-group stay in that group during their lifetime.

#### B. Death/Inheritance Transition Matrix ($D$)
If an agent dies (probability $\mu$), the descendant inherits a new $f$ based on the intergenerational matrix $F$ (`productivity.transition_F`). The offspring's initial life-cycle component $a$ is $a_L$ with probability $\pi = 0.9$ (`productivity.pi_newborn_low`) and $a_H$ with probability $1-\pi$. 
The rows of $D$ represent the current state of the parent $(f, a)$, and the columns represent the initial state of the child $(f', a')$.
- For rows $i=1, 2$ (parent has $f_L$):
  - $D_{i, 1} = F_{11} \cdot \pi$
  - $D_{i, 2} = F_{11} \cdot (1-\pi)$
  - $D_{i, 3} = F_{12} \cdot \pi$
  - $D_{i, 4} = F_{12} \cdot (1-\pi)$
- For rows $i=3, 4$ (parent has $f_H$):
  - $D_{i, 1} = F_{21} \cdot \pi$
  - $D_{i, 2} = F_{21} \cdot (1-\pi)$
  - $D_{i, 3} = F_{22} \cdot \pi$
  - $D_{i, 4} = F_{22} \cdot (1-\pi)$

#### C. Aggregate Transition Matrix ($P$)
The final transition matrix used in the Bellman equation and the law of motion for the distribution is:
$$P = (1-\mu)S + \mu D$$
This matrix governs the transition probability $P(z'|z)$ across model periods.

### 4. Capital Grid Construction
While the grid is defined in `numerical`, `calibration.py` logic must ensure the capital grid $k$ is optimized for the EGM:
- **Range:** From `k_min` (0.0, borrowing constraint) to `k_max` (100.0).
- **Density:** Use an exponential growth grid to ensure higher density near the borrowing constraint where the policy function $k'(k, z)$ is most non-linear.
  $$k_i = k_{min} + (k_{max} - k_{min}) \cdot \left( \frac{i}{N-1} \right)^{k\_grid\_exp}$$

### 5. Summary of Outputs for `EconomyConfig`
The calibration logic will populate the following attributes of the `EconomyConfig` object:
1.  `beta`: $\beta_{period}$ (0.822)
2.  `delta`: $\delta_{period}$ (0.341)
3.  `z_states`: $[0.463, 0.932, 1.197, 2.411]$
4.  `transition_matrix`: The 4x4 matrix $P$ computed above.
5.  `k_grid`: The $N=100$ point non-linear grid.
6.  `g_y_ratio`: Fixed at 0.17 as per Section 3.1.
