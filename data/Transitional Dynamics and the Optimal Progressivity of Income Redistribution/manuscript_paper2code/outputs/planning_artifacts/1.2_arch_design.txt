[Implementation approach]
We will implement a heterogeneous-agent general equilibrium model using the Aiyagari-Bewley-Huggett framework. Given the heavy computational load of solving policy functions over the transition path, we will use 'Numba' for JIT-compiled performance and 'Endogenous Grid Method' (EGM) for the savings policy. The system will be divided into: 1. A core physics engine for household optimization and Markov transitions; 2. A steady-state solver using nested fixed-point iterations for prices (r) and fiscal parameters (lambda); 3. A transition path solver using backward induction on value functions and forward simulation of the wealth distribution; 4. An optimizer that searches over the tax progressivity parameter (tau) to maximize specific welfare criteria.
